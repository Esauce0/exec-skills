# exec-communication

Communicate at the right altitude with ownership and judgment.

## Overview

Covers updates, recommendations, escalations, memos, promotion cases, board material, and live presence in consequential meetings, and pairs with exec-core's prose and posture standards.

Requires `exec-core` (standards, Brain protocol, doctrine library). Install the marketplace as a whole.

## Skills (2)

- **executive-communication**: Design and review communication to executives and boards for both substance and altitude: weekly updates, recommendations, decision requests, escalations, bad news, narrative memos, promotion cases, and board material. Chooses the right form for the reader's state (pyramid, narrative, shared diagnosis), separates what's decided from what's open, and decides what genuinely needs escalation.
- **meeting-presence**: Coach behavior in consequential live interactions with executives: knowing the meeting's purpose and decider, opening answer-first, handling challenge with composure, disagreeing cleanly, asking the question that reframes, knowing when to stop talking, avoiding the habits that cost more with seniority, and closing with decisions and owners. Distinguishes gravitas (judgment under pressure) from polish, and presence capability from conformity pressure.

## Commands (4)

- `/debrief-exec-meeting`: Debrief an executive meeting against its prep: what happened, whether the objective was met, signals from each person (with evidence class), the posture you showed, and what to do differently
- `/prepare-board-update`: Decide what belongs at board altitude versus operating detail, and reconstruct a board update or board slot around strategy, risk, talent, performance, and a clear ask
- `/prepare-exec-meeting`: Prepare for a consequential executive interaction: what each person likely cares about (from evidence), what to accomplish, own, recommend, ask, and avoid, and where to show next-level capability
- `/review-exec-update`: Review an executive update, email, escalation, or memo for both substance and communication quality: the thinking, the escalations, the posture it signals, and the prose, then rewrite it

## Install

```bash
claude plugin marketplace add <path-or-repo>/exec-skills
claude plugin install exec-communication@exec-skills
```
