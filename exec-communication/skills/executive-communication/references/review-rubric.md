# Review rubric for executive communication

Used by `/review-exec-update`. Rate each dimension strong / adequate / weak, with the specific line or omission that drove the rating. Substance comes before communication: a well-written update with the wrong substance is worse than a rough one with the right substance.

## Substance

| Dimension | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Point | One clear governing thought answering the reader's question | No point, or several competing ones |
| Implications | Every fact carries its "so what" for this reader | Activity reporting |
| Decisions | Decisions within scope are made and reported | Decisions within scope are asked for |
| Escalations | Only what needs the reader's authority, with recommendation, deadline, and cost of delay | Complaints, vague asks, or none when one is needed |
| Bad news | First, sized, with action taken | Buried, minimized, or missing |
| Ownership | Named owners and dates; first person for misses | "We're looking into it"; passive voice for misses |
| Enterprise link | Connects to the reader's and company's priorities | Only the writer's task list |
| Command of detail | One or two details that prove the writer looked underneath | Either no specifics or a dump of all of them |
| Confidence | Stated once, precisely | Hedging throughout, or false certainty |

## Communication

| Dimension | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| First two lines | Stand alone as the message | Throat-clearing, context, or meta |
| Structure | Hierarchy by importance; decided/open/asks separated | Chronology |
| Density | Everything earns its place; nothing decision-relevant missing | Padding, or cryptic over-compression |
| Form | Matches the reader's state and the channel | Wrong form for the reader (a wall of text to a listener) |
| Language | Plain, concrete, natural cadence | Corporate filler; AI tells; executive theater |
| Formatting | Minimal; bold only on the ask | Headings on short text; bold everywhere |

## Posture

Name the posture the draft signals (per exec-core `executive-posture`) and the markers that produce it. The fix for a posture problem is a behavior change first.

## Output order

1. Verdict in one sentence.
2. The one or two substance issues that matter most.
3. Escalation leaks, if any.
4. Posture read.
5. The rewrite.
6. The behavior to change next time.

Keep the critique shorter than the rewrite.
