# Meeting file schema

Path: `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-<person-or-forum>-<topic>.md`. Written by `/prepare-exec-meeting`; completed by `/debrief-exec-meeting`. The gap between the two halves is the most useful evidence the system collects.

```markdown
# <Meeting> — YYYY-MM-DD

## Prep
- Attendees and roles: [<Name>](../people/<slug>.md), ...
- Decider(s):
- Why this meeting matters to my trajectory:
- What each attendee likely cares about (with evidence):
- My objective (one sentence, observable):
- What I will own in the room:
- My recommendation(s):
- Questions I will ask:
- Likely challenges and my answers:
- What to avoid:
- Next-level opportunity in this meeting:
- Pre-wiring done (who, when, what they said):
- Success looks like (observable by end of meeting):

## Debrief
- What happened (observations):
- Objective met? (yes / partly / no, and evidence):
- Decisions and owners:
- Signals by attendee (trust, friction, interest), with evidence class:
- My posture (as shown):
- What I'd do differently:
- Follow-ups sent (date):
- Evidence entries created:
- Model updates proposed:
```
