# Using the system

## Week one

1. **Set up the Career Brain.** `/setup-career-brain`. Point it at your Product Brains when it asks. The most valuable things to get right early are your decision rights (in `self/current-role.md`), what your manager is measured on, and the revealed ladder: what the last few people promoted to your target level actually did.
2. **Feed it.** Paste or point at the last few weeks of 1:1 notes, feedback, and meeting notes: `/capture`. Capture the unflattering events too. A Brain that only holds wins produces a flattering, useless model.
3. **Use it on something real.** The next time you write to your VP, run `/review-exec-update` first. Before the next meeting that matters, `/prepare-exec-meeting`; afterward, `/debrief-exec-meeting`.
4. **Close the week.** `/review-my-week`.

## The recurring rhythm

| When | What |
|---|---|
| As things happen | `/capture` (two minutes, right after the event, beats a perfect write-up later) |
| Before consequential meetings | `/prepare-exec-meeting` |
| Within 24 hours after | `/debrief-exec-meeting` |
| Before sending anything important upward | `/review-exec-update` |
| Weekly | `/review-my-week` |
| First week of each month | `/review-my-month` (the routine place hypotheses change) |
| Every six months, and after any promotion, reorg, or manager change | `/career-retrospective` |
| Planning season, or when scope feels flat | `/expand-my-scope`, `/audit-my-time` |
| Ahead of a promotion cycle (a quarter or two out) | `/position-for-promotion` |

## What good output looks like

Good output is short and opens with the diagnosis and its confidence. Evidence is labeled as what happened, what's inferred, or a hypothesis about someone's view. It names a specific move with a date and says what would change the advice. If an answer would be the same for any PM at any company, the system has failed; tell it which facts it's missing, or capture them.

## What the system will do that you may not like

- Tell you a promotion case doesn't exist yet.
- Tell you a draft escalates decisions you should make yourself.
- Say your week was competent execution that didn't move your trajectory.
- Refuse to treat a friendly executive as a sponsor without evidence of advocacy.
- Refuse to state what someone "really thinks" of you as fact.
- Point out when you're overreaching.

These behaviors are intended, because the system's allegiance is to accurate diagnosis.

## Working with Product Brains

The system reads your pm-brain scaffolds (strategy, decisions, stakeholders, meetings, metrics) and never writes to them. When a workflow suggests recording a product decision, it points you to that brain's own workflow (for example `/decide`). Career analysis stays in the Career Brain.

## When to go to the doctrine directly

Ask "what does Grove say about..." or "is this more Pfeffer or Campbell?" and the doctrine library answers from the researched dossiers, including where sources disagree and which position fits your situation. It won't role-play anyone.
